About Northstar Wealth Partners - Uptown Charlotte

Northstar Wealth Partners - Uptown Charlotte is located in the South Tryon district near Uptown Charlotte and supports clients across Mecklenburg County and surrounding communities. The office provides in-person and virtual financial planning conversations for individuals, families, retirees, and business owners looking for guidance around long-term financial goals.

Clients commonly visit this location for retirement planning, portfolio reviews, investment guidance, and broader financial planning conversations. Advisors at this branch support both ongoing wealth management relationships and one-time planning discussions depending on client needs.

The office includes private consultation rooms, multilingual support, and online scheduling for added flexibility. Saturday hours are available for select appointment types.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment to visit this office?

Appointments are recommended for financial planning and advisory meetings, but clients can still stop by during lobby hours for basic banking support or questions.

Is parking available nearby?

Yes. Nearby public and garage parking options are available throughout the South Tryon and Uptown Charlotte area.

Can I meet with an advisor virtually?

Yes. Advisors at this office offer both in-person and virtual meetings based on your schedule and preferences.

What languages are supported at this office?

This location supports clients in English, Spanish, Chinese, and French.

Is this office accessible by public transit?

Yes. The office is near major Uptown transit routes and has accessible entrance and elevator access.
